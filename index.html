Most of what the franchisor needs takes two or three of our skill sets. The goal is not silos. It is knowing who drives each area, who supports, and what none of us should be doing at all.

Prepared by Claude Code from the session transcript and the role-mapping doc the team built live. Owns means you drive it and make the calls. Supports means you contribute your piece inside the owner's lane. Off the plate means it was explicitly flagged as outside your strengths or interest.

02 · Shared Lanes

Where we deliberately overlap

Three areas stay joint by design. The point is knowing which piece of each belongs to whom.

  • Franchisee recruitment and salesEvery franchisee goes through all three of us. GianCarlo and Brad create the attraction; Jared runs the formal process from expressed interest through qualification, interviews, and enrollment.
  • Marketing strategyGianCarlo leads. Brad brings scripting and on-camera presence. Jared brings the scientific side: analytics, testing, and AI-driven reporting. One strategy, three inputs.
  • Strategic planningBig-picture vision, goal setting, and budget allocation stay a three-person exercise, with Jared and GianCarlo driving the process.
03 · How We Operate

Three agreements from the session

The $5K rule

Budgets get set per initiative at strategic planning. Whoever owns the initiative spends freely under $5,000 within that budget. Anything above $5,000 is a team check-in. Autonomy without bottlenecks, oversight without hoops.

Zone of genius accountability

Early on we will all work outside our zones when we have to. But as we grow, we call each other out when someone is spending time outside their zone of genius when they do not have to. That time costs the company money and momentum.

AI runs the internal ops

Jared's AI stack automates the franchisor's internal operations. It doubles as a core differentiator in the franchise offering: custom tooling the established players are not offering their franchisees.

04 · Outsource + Open

What none of us should do, and what is unresolved

Outsource now

Video editing. Nobody's zone of genius, cheap to replace, and the highest-leverage time saver across all three of us. SEO follows the same logic; Brad holds it only until it is handed off.

Open items for the next session

Ongoing coaching systems need an owner; nothing is documented yet and Brad passed on it. Website and SEO need a long-term home. Trade shows are undecided: strong for franchisee recruiting, heavy on time.
