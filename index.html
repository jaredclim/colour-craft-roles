Who Owns What

Roles for Jared, GianCarlo, and Brad, defined in the August 13 partner session. Working draft for team feedback.

Jared Lim GianCarlo Lies Brad Samuels

Most of what the franchisor needs takes two or three of our skill sets. This maps who drives each area, who supports, and what nobody should be doing at all.

Prepared by Claude Code from the session transcript and the role-mapping doc the team built live. Owns means you drive it. Supports means you contribute inside the owner's lane. Off the plate means flagged as outside your strengths or interest.

01 · The Three Roles

One lane each, built on strengths

Each role maps to what came out of the strengths and interests round: where each person is strongest, most energized, and most useful to the franchisor over the next 12 to 18 months.

Jared · People, Systems & AI

Jared Lim

Zone of genius: recruiting and developing key people, filling gaps through people and systems, and quality control that raises the standard of everything the team ships. From discovery call until a franchisee is successful, Jared drives it.

Owns
The franchisee recruitment system once someone is interested: info call, application, interviews, and enrollment. Franchisee support and development strategy across the full system: the learning platform, live training systems, and ongoing coaching systems, with script writing and final editing oversight on training content. Ongoing system development and continuous improvement. Training and team development. The AI stack. Team meeting rhythm.
Supports
Script writing for recruitment marketing and social content. The analytical, scientific side of marketing. Financial analysis. Strategic planning.
Off the plate
Filming and editing execution. Bookkeeping and invoicing. Legal and administration.
GianCarlo · Marketing & New Markets

GianCarlo Lies

Zone of genius: leadership through vision and values, consultative sales across $30M+ sold, creative offers and promotions, and taking big bets and delivering on them.

Owns
Company-level marketing, end to end: the marketing that attracts clients. Organic social, Meta ads, and email marketing, from strategy through execution, including dispersing content across channels. The vision and values living in the company, kept front and center. New market launches: the launch playbook plus hands-on, in-market support when a franchisee opens new territory.
Supports
Franchisee marketing strategy across all channels. Franchisee recruitment and sales. Strategic planning.
Off the plate
Editing. Legal and administration.
Brad · Franchisee Marketing, Face of the Company, Finance & Legal

Brad Samuels

Zone of genius: creation and expression, backed by strong generalist range, initiating new things, and being good with money.

Owns
Franchisee marketing: the channels that generate franchisee leads and bring people to discovery and info calls. Lead content creator and face of the company: on video across franchisee recruitment content, brand content, and training content, plus stages and podcasts. Website and SEO. Finances. Legal and administration.
Supports
Live facilitation of franchisee sessions, which all three partners share. Franchisee recruitment and sales. Ongoing system development with Jared.
Off the plate
Email marketing. New market launches. Ongoing coaching systems. Day-to-day management.
02 · Shared Lanes

Where we deliberately overlap

Three areas stay joint by design.

  • Franchisee recruitment and salesEvery franchisee is interviewed by all three of us, at least in the early stages. Brad's marketing generates the leads and info calls; Jared owns the system that takes them from interest to enrollment.
  • ContentThree surfaces, different pairings. Social organic: GianCarlo owns the strategy, Jared writes scripts, Brad is on camera, a hired editor cuts, GianCarlo disperses across channels. Training platform content: mostly Jared and Brad. Franchisee marketing content: mostly Brad and GianCarlo.
  • Strategic planningBig-picture vision, goal setting, and budget allocation stay a three-person exercise.
03 · Outsource

What none of us should do

Outsource now

Video editing. Outside everyone's zone of genius and cheap to replace.
